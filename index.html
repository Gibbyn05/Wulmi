Questions

Is Wulmi available yet?

Not yet. Wulmi is currently in development for iPhone.

How does the pause work?

You will choose the apps and websites where you want a short pause before buying. The details are still being built and tested.

What does Wulmi count?

Only the purchases you choose to log yourself. That is the idea behind the app.

How can I follow along?

Write to hello@wulmi.com and we will keep you posted.
